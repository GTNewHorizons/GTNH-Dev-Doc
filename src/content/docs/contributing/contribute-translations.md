---
title: Contribute translations
description: Join a language team, report a translation issue, or organize a new GTNH translation.
---

To translate the pack, join your language team's project on **ParaTranz**, an
online translation platform.
[GTNH-Translations](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GTNH-Translations) lists
the projects and maintains the contribution instructions.

## Join an existing language team

Open the README's
[translator instructions](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GTNH-Translations#for-translators)
and follow the ParaTranz link for your language. Sign in with your GitHub
account to join the project, then contribute there following your team's
translation and review guidance. Translations are exported to
GTNH-Translations automatically.

To coordinate with the team on the [GTNH Discord](https://discord.gg/EXshrPV),
open the `#translation-dev` forum channel. Read its **How to Contribute** post
and find your language's thread.

Keep language threads focused on translation work. Spam and off-topic posts
are subject to moderation.

## Report a translation issue or suggestion

For incorrect or missing translations, localization requests, or suggestions
about specific text,
[open an issue in GTNH-Translations](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GTNH-Translations/issues/new/choose).
Choose the affected language's template, or **Other** if the issue isn't
specific to one language. Describe the affected text, where you found it,
and any suggested wording.

## Start a new language team

A new language must meet these requirements before it can have a thread in
`#translation-dev`:

- At least **three translators**, including a **lead translator**.
- A **ParaTranz project** for the language.
- The designated **GTNH account added to the project's admin group** so the
  GTNH team can maintain access if the lead becomes unavailable.

Once you've formed your team, follow the repository's
[new-language instructions](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GTNH-Translations#i-want-to-make-contribution-to-language-that-is-not-listed-here)
to contact the translation maintainer and arrange project setup and a Discord
thread.
